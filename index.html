Rebuilding Mindvalley's CRM and filling the lifecycle gaps

Mindvalley, 2024 to present

Mindvalley's CRM had grown one campaign at a time across multiple markets and channels. I rebuilt it into one system: clean data and events, shared segments, and trigger-based journeys that each have a clear job.

Then I added the journeys that were missing, starting with cart and browse abandonment, so the moments where customers hesitate get a response instead of silence. Every journey runs against a control group.

+33%improvement in free-to-paid conversion after the rebuild

How the lifecycle works

  1. Sign-up and welcomePersonalised by how someone first arrived: a person who came in through manifesting content gets manifesting programs recommended first
  2. TrialNudges toward first value in the free experience
  3. Reactivation and recommendationsA freemium recommendation engine that brings quiet users back and pushes them toward the right paid program
  4. Browse and cart abandonmentNew journeys that follow up on hesitation
  5. Purchase and onboardingGetting new members into their first program
  6. Win-backRe-engagement for lapsed members

SOCAR Monthly Pass

SOCAR Malaysia, 2023. Project manager and CRM manager

The Monthly Pass is a subscription that unlocks 50% off up to 99 drives, valid for 30 days. The aim was to turn occasional renters into habitual ones.

I was project manager and CRM manager for the team that built it. I also built the automation behind every purchase: the web page talks to the payment tracker through its API, the CRM confirms the purchase with the customer, and the promo code that unlocks the discount is issued automatically.

RM 1Mrevenue in the first month. 62% of pass holders bought the pass again, and pass holders spent 11% more overall

Launching TREVO, then rebranding it and reworking its CRM

SOCAR and TREVO, 2021 to 2024

TREVO is a peer-to-peer car sharing platform, where car owners rent out their own cars. I started its lifecycle from scratch, beginning with the basic steps every owner and renter has to go through.

It was a heavily data- and intuition-driven project. I put understanding the user experience first, so I didn't spend time building journeys that wouldn't matter.

When TREVO's corporate identity was refreshed, I reworked the CRM to match: new templates, a rebuilt journey for car owners and renters, and messaging that carried the new brand consistently.

+15%first-booking-to-MAU conversion, driving 10% year-on-year GMV growth
